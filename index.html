A little space for making

Why I keep
this journal.

I like making things, and I rarely stick to one medium for long. I might be working on crochet one week and reach for fineliners or markers the next. Painting, sketching, and graphic design all give me something different to return to. julia.stan is my place to bring those interests together and keep a record of what I actually make.

A photo can hold onto a stage of a project that is easy to forget once the next stage takes over. The first lines of a drawing, a small change in color, or a section of crochet can be worth keeping alongside a finished piece. I want to be able to look back at those moments, remember when I worked on them, and see how my work changes over time.

Some entries will have a little explanation. Others will just have a title, a date, and a photo. Both belong here. If several sessions are part of the same piece, a project label will keep them connected.

The craft buttons above show the different days I have documented for each medium. Further down, you can watch my drawing process and explore places around Los Angeles and Orange County that could inspire a future project. For everything I have shared so far, head to the Gallery.
