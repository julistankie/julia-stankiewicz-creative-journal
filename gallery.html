Notes on the collection

What I have
been making.

This gallery brings together my fineliner drawings and acrylic marker experiments. Entries appear from newest to oldest, with month headings to make the collection easier to browse. Each photograph has a title and the date of the creative session. The date describes when I worked on the piece, which may be earlier than the day I added the image to this website.

A project can include several entries. One photograph might show the beginning of a drawing, while another records a later stage or a finished result. Giving those entries the same project label helps me revisit the sequence without searching through everything else. Individual sketches and small experiments can also stand on their own. They do not need a larger project or a long explanation to belong here.

The craft filter lets visitors focus on a medium such as acrylic markers or fineliner drawing. The project filter narrows the collection to entries that belong together. Using both filters makes a growing archive easier to explore. Choosing All brings the full collection back into view.

Some entries include a short note about what I tried, what I learned, or what I want to change next time. Those notes appear only when I add them. I want the photographs to remain the main focus while leaving enough room to record the details that make a particular session worth remembering.
